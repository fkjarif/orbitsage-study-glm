Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

OrbitSage/Study ("the Service") is designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle — or rather, don't handle — your data.

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers. Everything stays in your browser.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • API Key: Your OpenRouter API key is stored exclusively in your browser's IndexedDB (via Dexie.js). It is never sent to any server other than OpenRouter's API endpoint.
  • Chat History: All conversation messages are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. They are never transmitted to any server other than OpenRouter for AI processing.
  • Images: Uploaded images are compressed client-side and sent directly to OpenRouter's API as base64 data. They are not stored permanently anywhere — not on our servers, not in your browser after the session.

3. Data Transmission

The only external service your data is sent to is OpenRouter (openrouter.ai). Specifically:

  • Your API key is sent as an authorization header to OpenRouter's API.
  • Your chat messages (including any images) are sent to OpenRouter for AI model processing.
  • OpenRouter's own Privacy Policy governs how they handle your data.

No data is sent to any other server, including our own. This application has no backend.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies, web beacons, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking technologies. The only browser storage used is IndexedDB for your API key and chat history, and localStorage for remembering your acknowledgment of the security warning.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service communicates with:

  • OpenRouter API — for AI model inference. See OpenRouter's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — for loading web fonts (Inter, JetBrains Mono). Google may collect IP addresses and other data per their Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

While we take reasonable measures to ensure the security of data within the browser:

  • Data stored in IndexedDB is accessible to anyone with physical or remote access to your device.
  • Malicious browser extensions could potentially access data stored in your browser.
  • We recommend setting a spending limit on your OpenRouter account to mitigate potential losses from key compromise.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Key" feature within the Service.
  • Clearing your browser's site data (IndexedDB, localStorage) for this domain.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please reach out through the Service's website.
